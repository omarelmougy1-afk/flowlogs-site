Support

Questions, problems and security reports about Flowlogs go to one email address.

Email

omarelmougy1@gmail.com

Hours

Sunday to Thursday, 09:00 to 17:00 Cairo time, excluding public holidays.

First response

Within two business days.

Please include

  • your Jira Cloud site URL;
  • the Flowlogs screen (Flow report, Daily activity, or Saved views) and the date range;
  • steps to reproduce, and what you expected to happen;
  • a screenshot, with sensitive information removed where possible;
  • the approximate time and time zone of the problem.

Never send passwords, API tokens, or private keys.

Quick checks

  1. Make sure you can browse the affected project and its worklogs in Jira itself.
  2. Flowlogs counts each worklog on the day it was logged in Jira. Check the date range with that in mind.
  3. Clear the project, team, and user filters.
  4. Press Refresh once to bypass the short report cache.
  5. If only one person sees a problem, compare their Jira project permissions with someone who does not.
